Two-stage water-vapor (R718) high-temperature heat pump

This page is the interactive companion to the project's markdown documents (docs/01…10). It models a two-stage vapor-compression heat pump using water (H₂O, R718) as the working fluid: an evaporator (蒸发器) absorbs heat from a ~20 °C source at a deep-vacuum evaporating pressure, two compressor stages (低压/高压压缩机) with an economizer (经济器) vapor-injection stage in between lift it by a pressure ratio of ≈ 190, and the condenser (冷凝器) delivers heat to process water heated from 115 °C to ≈ 120 °C. A recuperator (回热器) dries the suction vapor; a main and a secondary expansion valve (主/次膨胀阀) close the loop.

Why water? GWP = 0, ODP = 0, non-flammable, free, critical point at 373.9 °C — ideal above 100 °C where halocarbons run out of headroom. Why is it hard? Deep vacuum on the cold side (1 % of atmospheric — every 100 Pa of suction-line pressure drop costs ≈ 1.2 K of lift), enormous suction volume flows, and steam's "drying" isentropes: compressing saturated 10 °C vapor at ratio 13.8 lands near 261 °C isentropic / ≈ 370–390 °C real — so interstage desuperheating (economizer injection plus liquid spray) is not an optimization but a feasibility requirement.

State numbering. The original task figure labels two different points "T10". This project renumbers the evaporator inlet as state 11; economizer vapor outlet keeps state 10. Loop: 1 → 2 (recuperator) → 3 (LP discharge) → 4 (after injection/spray mixing) → 5 (HP discharge) → 6 (condensate) → 7 (economizer out, branch tap) → 8 (recuperator out) → 11 (evaporator in); branch 7 → 9 (secondary valve) → 10 (injection vapor).

3 · Model summary

Full derivations live in docs 02–07; these are the closure equations the calculator implements.

Properties — IAPWS-IF97 (Regions 4, 2, 1)

Saturation both ways (R4), superheated-vapor Gibbs energy (R2: h, s, v, cp consistently from one function), compressed liquid (R1). The engine below implements the official coefficients and self-tests against the IF97 release verification tables on load (badge, top right).

Compressors

(C-1)ṁ = ηvol · ρsuc · Vdisp · N     (C-2) hout = hin + h(Pout, sin) − hinηis

Economizer + injection ratio (branch taps at 7, h₉ = h₇)

(E-1)ṁc(h₆ − h₇) = ṁinj(h₁₀ − h₉)  ⇒  r = h₆ − h₇h₁₀ − h₆ (no spray)

Interstage mixing — an enthalpy balance, never a temperature average

(M-1)ṁeh₃ + ṁinjh₁₀ + ṁsprayh₇ = ṁch₄,   ṁc = ṁe(1 + r + ws)

With vapor injection alone (r ≈ 0.13) the mix cools only ≈ 30 K; the spray term ws ≈ 0.25–0.28 brings state 4 to Tsat(Pint) + 15 K and is solved jointly with r from the two linear balances (doc 05 §5.5).

Recuperator (both sides carry ṁe; split is upstream at state 7)

(R-1)h₂ − h₁ = h₇ − h₈ = εrec · cp,v · (T₇ − T₁)

Valves & flash quality

(V-1)h₁₁ = h₈,   x₁₁ = h₈ − hf(Te)hfg(Te)  (0.186 saturated → ≈ 0.08 subcooled; >99.9 % vapor by volume)

Performance

(P-1)COPgas = ṁc(h₅ − h₆)ṁe(h₃ − h₂) + ṁc(h₅ − h₄),  COPel = ηdrive · COPgas,  COPCarnot = TcTc − Te

Energy closure Q̇cond = Q̇evap + Ẇ₁ + Ẇ₂ holds identically if every component balance holds (telescoping proof, doc 06 §6.8) — the calculator displays the numerical residual as a live check. Pint note: at runtime the intermediate pressure is emergent (intersection of the two compressor characteristics); specifying it here is design-mode reasoning (doc 06 §6.7).

Layered architecture

  • L0 · Interlocks (hardwired): high T₅, high P₄/PRV, loss of water flow, freeze guard (source outlet ≥ 3 °C — R718 cannot evaporate below 0.01 °C), E-stop. Latching, manual reset.
  • L1 · Regulatory PI/PID: SIMC/IMC tunings from identified FOPDT fits; back-calculation anti-windup on every loop; derivative only on SH (filtered); rate limits on speed; gain scheduling over load & pressure ratio; high/low-select overrides (T₅ → spray/capacity, P₁ → N₁).
  • L2 · Supervisory: Pint setpoint from the off-design COP map, superheat-setpoint adaptation, source-pump COP optimization (pumping power vs Te gain ≈ +2.5 %/K).
  • L3 · Optional offset-free linear MPC as a setpoint governor: 10-state linearized model (doc 08), Ts = 2–5 s, prediction 60–120 samples, constraints = the envelope table; worth it under frequent large load swings, otherwise decentralized PID suffices.

Sequencing highlights

Startup: evacuate < 100 Pa → leak-rate hold → deaerated charge → water loops on → LP at minimum speed with feedforward valve opening → build Pint → start HP → bumpless handover → ramp supply setpoint to 120 °C. Shutdown retains vacuum (isolation valves). Low-source mode replaces "defrost": reduced-capacity envelope with the freeze interlock, since sub-zero evaporation is impossible for water.
